Rate over time

Beat error is half the difference between the tick→tock and tock→tick times; a perfectly even clock reads 0. Rate is measured against the sound card’s clock, which is typically good to a few tens of ppm (a couple of seconds a day), so treat differences smaller than that with care. Place the phone/mic near the clock case and keep other noise down. Add ?demo to the URL to see synthetic data.
